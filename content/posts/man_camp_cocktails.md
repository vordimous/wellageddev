> AI-assisted: I picked these recipes from two of my Mixel cocktail lists and filtered them against the bottles and pantry I'm bringing to camp. Claude helped match recipes to ingredients and format the menu.

Every drink here can be made from the same bar: bourbon, rye, gin, tequila, mezcal, Champagne, sweet and dry vermouth, Chartreuse, St-Germain, Campari, Aperol, and a few basics. Where a recipe calls for something we don't have, a swap is noted.

## Bourbon

### Boston Sour
*Whiskey Sour W/ Egg*

Whiskey (rye or bourbon), lemon juice, simple syrup, salt, egg (white, large)

*tart, creamy*

### French 95

Bourbon whiskey, lemon juice, simple syrup, orange juice, Champagne (top)

*dry, tart*

### Gold Rush

Bourbon whiskey, lemon juice, honey syrup (3:1 by vol.)

*sweet, tart*

### Hot Toddy

Whiskey, honey syrup, lemon, water (boiling)

*hot*

### Kentucky Buck

Angostura bitters, simple syrup, lemon juice, strawberry, Bourbon whiskey, ginger beer (to top), lemon, strawberry

*fresh, herbal*

### Kentucky Maid

Bourbon whiskey, lime juice, simple syrup, mint, cucumber, mint, cucumber

*tart, sweet*

### Kentucky Mule

Bourbon whiskey, lime juice, ginger beer, lime, mint

*fresh, herbal*

### Negroni, American
*Murica*

Bourbon whiskey, Cocchi di Torino, Aperol, Campari, Angostura bitters, lemon

*sweet, bitter*

**Swap:** sweet vermouth for Cocchi di Torino.

### Old Fashioned

Bourbon whiskey, Angostura bitters, bar sugar (cube), orange, cherry

*herbal, strong*

### Old Fashioned, Simple

Bourbon whiskey, simple syrup, Angostura bitters, orange

*herbal, strong*

### Old-Fashioned, D&C Ideal
*"Our Ideal" From Cocktail Codex*

Bourbon whiskey (Elijah Craig Small Batch), demerara syrup (or 1 tsp rich Dem. gum syrup), Angostura bitters, aromatic bitters (Bitter Truth's), lemon, orange

*herbal, strong*

**Swap:** rich simple syrup (2:1) for demerara syrup, an extra dash of Angostura for the aromatic bitters.

### Strawberry Fix

Strawberry (quartered), lemon juice, simple syrup, Bourbon whiskey, strawberry

*sweet, tart*

### The Stark

Lemon juice, honey syrup (3:1 by vol.), yellow Chartreuse, Bourbon whiskey, Angostura bitters (to float)

*bitter, herbal*

### Whiskey Sour

Whiskey (Bourbon or Rye), lemon juice, simple syrup, orange, cherry, egg (white only)

*tart, sweet*

### Whiskey Sour, Everleigh

*creamy, tart*
